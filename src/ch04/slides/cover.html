Chapter 4

Understanding Ownership

Ownership is Rust’s most unique feature and has deep implications for the rest of the language. It enables Rust to make memory safety guarantees without needing a garbage collector, so it’s important to understand how ownership works. This chapter covers ownership as well as several related features: borrowing, slices, and how Rust lays data out in memory.
