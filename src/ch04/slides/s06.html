Chapter 4 · 4.1 What Is Ownership?

The ownership rules

First, the ownership rules. Keep them in mind as we work through the examples that illustrate them:

Ownership rules.

  1. Each value in Rust has an owner.
  2. There can only be one owner at a time.
  3. When the owner goes out of scope, the value will be dropped.
