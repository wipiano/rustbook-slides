Chapter 5 · 5.2 An Example Program Using Structs

Why tuples invite errors; labeling with structs

  • Mixing up width and height wouldn’t matter for the area calculation, but it would if we wanted to draw the rectangle on the screen!
  • We would have to keep in mind that width is the tuple index 0 and height is the tuple index 1.
  • That would be even harder for someone else to figure out and keep in mind if they used our code.
  • Because we haven’t conveyed the meaning of our data in our code, it’s now easier to introduce errors.

Refactoring with structs
