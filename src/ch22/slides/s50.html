Appendix · G How Rust is Made and Nightly Rust

The trains: stable ships, the next beta departs

4. Six weeks after the first beta was created, it’s time for a stable release! The stable branch is produced from the beta branch:

nightly: * - - * - - * - - * - - * - - * - * - *

                     |

beta:                * - - - - - - - - *

                                       |

stable:                                *

5. Hooray! Rust 1.5 is done! But because the six weeks have gone by, a new beta of the next version of Rust, 1.6, is needed too. So after stable branches off of beta, the next version of beta branches off of nightly again:

nightly: * - - * - - * - - * - - * - - * - * - *

                     |                         |

beta:                * - - - - - - - - *       *

                                       |

stable:                                *
