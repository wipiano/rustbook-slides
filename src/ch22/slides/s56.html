The RFC process and teams

So how do you learn about these new features? Rust’s development model follows a Request For Comments (RFC) process.

Definition A.22 (RFC). A written proposal for an improvement in Rust. Anyone can write RFCs to improve Rust.

The proposals are reviewed and discussed by the Rust team, which is comprised of many topic subteams. There’s a full list of the teams on Rust’s website (rust-lang.org/governance), including teams for each area of the project: language design, compiler implementation, infrastructure, documentation, and more.

The appropriate team reads the proposal and the comments, writes some comments of their own, and eventually there’s consensus to accept or reject the feature.
