Appendix summary

The appendices are reference material to come back to:

  • A — keywords in use and reserved; r# raw identifiers let you use any keyword as a name.
  • B — every operator (with its overloading trait) and every non-operator symbol.
  • C — the derivable traits: Debug, PartialEq/Eq, PartialOrd/Ord, Clone/Copy, Hash, Default.
  • D — cargo fmt, cargo fix, cargo clippy, and rust-analyzer for IDEs.
  • E — editions (2015, 2018, 2021, 2024) are opt-in via Cargo.toml and interoperate freely.
  • F — community translations of the book in many languages.
  • G — nightly, beta and stable ride six-week trains; unstable features stay behind feature flags on nightly; RFCs drive new features.

Remark. This is the end of the book. From here, keep the standard library documentation (rustup doc) and The Rust Edition Guide close at hand, and follow new features as they ride the trains to stable.
