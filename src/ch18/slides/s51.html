Draft posts without a content method

We still enable the creation of new posts in the draft state using Post::new, and the ability to add text to the post’s content.

But instead of a content method on a draft post that returns an empty string, draft posts won’t have a content method at all. That way, trying to get a draft post’s content gives a compiler error telling us the method doesn’t exist. As a result, it is impossible to accidentally display draft post content in production, because that code won’t even compile.
