Chapter 15 · 15.1 Using Box<T> to Point to Data on the Heap

The three use cases, in more detail

  1. Unknown size. Demonstrated below in “Enabling Recursive Types with Boxes.”
  2. Large data. Transferring ownership of a large amount of data can take a long time because the data is copied around on the stack. To improve performance, store the data on the heap in a box: then only the small amount of pointer data is copied around on the stack, while the data it references stays in one place on the heap.
  3. Trait implemented. This case is known as a trait object; “Using Trait Objects to Abstract over Shared Behavior” in Chapter 18 is devoted to it — so what you learn here you’ll apply again there.
