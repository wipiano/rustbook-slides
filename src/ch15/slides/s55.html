Chapter 15 · 15.5 RefCell<T> and the Interior Mutability Pattern

Choosing Box<T>, Rc<T>, or RefCell<T>

A recap of the reasons to choose each type:

Table 15.2: Ownership and borrowing of the three smart pointers

TypeOwnersBorrows allowedBorrows checked at
Box<T>singleimmutable or mutablecompile time
Rc<T>multipleimmutable onlycompile time
RefCell<T>singleimmutable or mutableruntime

Because RefCell<T> allows mutable borrows checked at runtime, you can mutate the value inside the RefCell<T> even when the RefCell<T> is immutable. Mutating the value inside an immutable value is the interior mutability pattern. Next: a situation where it is useful, and how it’s possible.
