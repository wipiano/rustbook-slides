Chapter summary

Well done! You’ve made it to the end of the book. In this final project you:

  • listened for TCP connections with TcpListener, read HTTP request lines through a BufReader, and wrote raw HTTP responses (status line, Content-Length, body) to the TcpStream;
  • served hello.html for / and 404.html for anything else, and saw how a slow /sleep request blocks a single-threaded server;
  • built a ThreadPool by compiler-driven development: Workers fed through a channel whose receiver is shared as Arc<Mutex<Receiver<Job>>>;
  • shut the pool down gracefully with Drop: dropping the sender closes the channel, the Workers break out of their loops, and join waits for them.

Thank you for joining this tour of Rust. You’re now ready to implement your own Rust projects and help with other people’s.
