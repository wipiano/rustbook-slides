Sending the job — and receiving it

After creating a new Job instance from the closure we get in execute, we send that job down the sending end of the channel.

  • We call unwrap on send for the case that sending fails. That might happen if, for example, we stop all our threads from executing, meaning the receiving end has stopped receiving new messages.
  • At the moment we can’t stop our threads from executing: they continue as long as the pool exists. We use unwrap because we know the failure case won’t happen, but the compiler doesn’t know that.

But we’re not quite done yet! In the Worker, the closure passed to thread::spawn still only references the receiving end of the channel. Instead, the closure needs to loop forever, asking the receiving end of the channel for a job and running the job when it gets one. Listing 21.20 makes that change to Worker::new.
