Chapter 11

Writing Automated Tests

In his 1972 essay “The Humble Programmer,” Edsger W. Dijkstra said that “program testing can be a very effective way to show the presence of bugs, but it is hopelessly inadequate for showing their absence.” That doesn’t mean we shouldn’t try to test as much as we can!

Testing is a complex skill: one chapter cannot cover every detail of writing good tests, so this chapter covers the mechanics of Rust’s testing facilities — the annotations and macros available when writing tests, the default behavior and options for running them, and how to organize tests into unit tests and integration tests.
