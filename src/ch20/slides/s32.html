Chapter 20 · 20.2 Advanced Traits

Defining traits with associated types

Traits were first covered in Chapter 10 (“Defining Shared Behavior with Traits”), without the more advanced details. Now we can get into the nitty-gritty.

Definition 20.12 (Associated type). A type placeholder connected with a trait, so that the trait’s method definitions can use it in their signatures. The implementor of the trait specifies the concrete type used in place of the placeholder for the particular implementation. That way a trait can use some types without needing to know exactly what they are until the trait is implemented.

Most of the advanced features in this chapter are rarely needed. Associated types are somewhere in the middle: they’re used more rarely than features explained in the rest of the book, but more commonly than many of the other features discussed in this chapter.
