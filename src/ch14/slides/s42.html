Chapter 14 · 14.3 Cargo Workspaces

Publishing workspace crates, and why workspaces

Publishing

If you publish the crates in the workspace to crates.io, each crate in the workspace must be published separately. As with cargo test, publish a particular crate of the workspace with the -p flag and the name of the crate you want to publish.

Example 14.9. For additional practice, add an add_two crate to this workspace in a similar way to the add_one crate!

When to use a workspace

  • As your project grows, consider a workspace: it lets you work with smaller, easier-to-understand components than one big blob of code.
  • Keeping the crates in a workspace can make coordination between them easier if they are often changed at the same time.
