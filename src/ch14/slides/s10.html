Chapter 14 · 14.2 Publishing a Crate to Crates.io

Exporting a convenient public API

The structure of your public API is a major consideration when publishing a crate. People who use your crate are less familiar with the structure than you are, and might have difficulty finding the pieces they want to use if your crate has a large module hierarchy.

Chapter 7 covered making items public with the pub keyword and bringing items into a scope with use. But the structure that makes sense to you while developing a crate might not be convenient for your users. If you organize your structs in a hierarchy of multiple levels, people who want a type defined deep in it might have trouble finding out that the type exists. They might also be annoyed at having to enter use my_crate::some_module::another_module::UsefulType; rather than use my_crate::UsefulType;.

The good news: if the structure isn’t convenient for others to use from another library, you don’t have to rearrange your internal organization. Instead, you can re-export items with pub use to make a public structure that’s different from your private structure.

Definition 14.5 (Re-exporting). Taking a public item in one location and making it public in another location, as if it were defined in the other location instead.
