Chapter 17 · Parallelism and Concurrency

When work becomes serial

In both workflows, you might have to coordinate between tasks. Maybe you thought the task assigned to one person was totally independent from everyone else’s work, but it actually requires another person on the team to finish their task first. Some of the work could be done in parallel, but some of it was actually serial.

Definition 17.7 (Serial). Work that can only happen in a series, one task after the other.

Likewise, you might realize that one of your own tasks depends on another of your tasks. Now your concurrent work has also become serial.
