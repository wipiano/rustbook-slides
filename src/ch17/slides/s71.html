That satisfies our goals for the types. Now the behavior: we want to race the future passed in against the duration. We can use trpl::sleep to make a timer future from the duration, and use trpl::select to run that timer with the future the caller passes in.

In Listing 17.20, we implement timeout by matching on the result of awaiting trpl::select.
