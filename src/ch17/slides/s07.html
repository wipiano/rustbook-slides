Chapter 17 · Parallelism and Concurrency

Parallelism: one task per person

When the team splits up a group of tasks by having each member take one task and work on it alone, this is parallelism. Each person on the team can make progress at the exact same time.

Definition 17.6 (Parallelism). Each worker takes one task and works on it alone, so progress on the different tasks happens at the exact same time.
