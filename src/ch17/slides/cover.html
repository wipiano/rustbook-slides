Fundamentals of Asynchronous Programming: Async, Await, Futures, and Streams

Many operations take a while to finish; asynchronous programming lets us express code as potential pausing points and eventual results. Building on Chapter 16’s threads, this chapter introduces Rust’s futures, streams, the async and await syntax, and the crates that implement async runtimes.
