The --snip-- stands for the same tx_fut body as in Listing 17.13 (with 1-second sleeps). We put each future within a Box to make them into trait objects, just as in Chapter 12’s “Returning Errors from run” section (trait objects are covered in detail in Chapter 18). Trait objects let us treat each of the anonymous futures produced by these types as the same type, because all of them implement the Future trait.
