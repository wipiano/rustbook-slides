Chapter 17 · 17.5 A Closer Look at the Traits for Async

The Future trait

We’ve used the Future, Stream, and StreamExt traits without details of how they work or fit together, which is fine most of the time. Sometimes, though, you’ll need more of their details, along with the Pin type and the Unpin trait. This section digs in just enough for those scenarios, leaving the really deep dive to other documentation.

Here’s how Rust defines the Future trait:

use std::pin::Pin;

use std::task::{Context, Poll};

 

pub trait Future {

    type Output;

 

    fn poll(self: Pin<&mut Self>, cx: &mut Context<'_>) -> Poll<Self::Output>;

}

That definition includes a bunch of new types and some syntax we haven’t seen before, so let’s walk through it piece by piece.
